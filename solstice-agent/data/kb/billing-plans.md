# Plans and pricing

Solstice offers four plans. The Free plan supports up to 5 members and includes core docs, tasks, and 5 GB of file storage. The Starter plan costs $9 per user per month and raises storage to 25 GB per workspace with unlimited guests. The Pro plan costs $18 per user per month and includes 100 GB of storage, advanced permissions, analytics, and priority email support. The Enterprise plan has custom pricing and adds SAML SSO, SCIM provisioning, audit logs, EU data residency, and a dedicated success manager.

## Annual billing

Paying annually gives a 20% discount on Starter and Pro. Annual invoices are issued once at the start of the term. Switching from monthly to annual takes effect immediately and the unused portion of the current month is credited.

## Adding and removing seats

Seats are prorated to the day. When you add a member mid-cycle you are charged only for the remainder of the cycle. When you remove a member, the unused time is credited to your next invoice. Seat changes on annual plans generate a prorated invoice or credit at the time of the change.

## Where to find billing history

Workspace admins can view and download all past invoices under Settings → Billing → Invoice history. Each invoice includes a PDF and a CSV line-item export. Receipts are also emailed to the billing contact when a charge succeeds.

## Payment methods

We accept all major credit cards, and ACH debit for annual Pro and Enterprise contracts over $5,000. Failed payments are retried three times over 10 days before the workspace is downgraded to read-only.
