# Passwords and account access

## Resetting your password

Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link we email is valid for 24 hours and can be used once. If you signed up with Google or SSO you will not have a password; use your identity provider instead.

## Account lockout

After 10 failed sign-in attempts, the account is locked for 30 minutes. Admins cannot unlock accounts early — the lockout is a fixed security control. Repeated lockouts on the same account trigger an email alert to the account owner.

## Two-factor authentication

Every plan supports TOTP-based two-factor authentication (Google Authenticator, 1Password, etc.). When you enable 2FA you receive 10 single-use recovery codes; store them somewhere safe. Losing both your authenticator and recovery codes requires an identity verification process with support that takes 3 to 5 business days — we cannot bypass it faster.

## Changing your email address

Change your sign-in email under Account → Profile. Both the old and new address receive a confirmation email, and the change completes only after the new address is verified. Workspace membership and page ownership are unaffected.
