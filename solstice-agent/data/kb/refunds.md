# Refund policy

Monthly subscriptions can be refunded within 14 days of the charge, no questions asked. Annual subscriptions have a longer window: 30 days from the charge date. After the window closes we can offer account credit at our discretion but not a card refund.

## How to request a refund

Workspace admins request refunds from Settings → Billing → Invoice history: open the invoice and click "Request refund". You do not need to contact support for eligible refunds — the flow is self-serve. Refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## What is not refundable

One-time seat proration charges, marketplace add-on purchases, and usage-based API overage fees are not refundable. Downgrading mid-term on an annual plan produces account credit rather than a refund.

## Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your bank instead of requesting a refund, your workspace is automatically suspended while the dispute is open. Contact billing@solstice.app to resolve it faster.
