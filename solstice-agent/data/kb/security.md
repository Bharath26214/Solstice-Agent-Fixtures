# Security and compliance

All customer data is encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or higher. Encryption keys are managed in a dedicated KMS with automatic annual rotation.

## Certifications

Solstice is SOC 2 Type II certified, with reports available under NDA to Enterprise customers and to prospects in active evaluation. We are GDPR compliant and offer a standard Data Processing Agreement to all paid plans. HIPAA BAAs are not currently offered.

## Data residency

By default, customer data is stored in the United States (us-east). Enterprise customers can choose EU data residency (eu-central) at workspace creation; existing workspaces can be migrated to the EU region by arrangement with support, with a scheduled maintenance window.

## Access controls

Production access is limited to a small on-call group, gated by hardware security keys, and every access is logged. Customer content is never used to train machine-learning models. Subprocessors are listed at solstice.app/subprocessors and customers on any paid plan can subscribe to change notifications.

## Vulnerability reporting

Report vulnerabilities to security@solstice.app. We run a private bug bounty; researchers who report in good faith are eligible for rewards and safe-harbor protections.
