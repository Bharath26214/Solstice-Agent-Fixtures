# Storage and upload limits

Workspace storage quotas by plan: Free includes 5 GB, Starter 25 GB, Pro 100 GB, and Enterprise 1 TB (expandable on request). Quota is measured against original uploaded files; previews and thumbnails we generate do not count.

## Per-file limits

The maximum size for a single uploaded file is 250 MB on Free and Starter, and 2 GB on Pro and Enterprise. Files are virus-scanned at upload; files that cannot be scanned (encrypted archives) are quarantined and the uploader is notified.

## What happens when you hit your quota

Uploads fail with a clear error, but nothing existing is deleted and the workspace keeps working. Admins see a usage breakdown by member and by project under Settings → General → Storage, and can free space by deleting large attachments or emptying the workspace trash — items in trash still count against quota until purged. Trash is purged automatically after 30 days.

## Version history

Page version history does not count against storage quota. Version history retention is 7 days on Free, 90 days on Starter and Pro, and unlimited on Enterprise.
