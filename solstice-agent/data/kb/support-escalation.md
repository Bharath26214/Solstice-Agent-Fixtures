# Contacting support

## How to open a support ticket

Click the "?" icon in the bottom-left of the app and choose "Contact support", or email help@solstice.app. Include your workspace URL and, for sync or performance issues, the diagnostic log export. You will receive a ticket ID by email (formatted like TKT-1234) that you can reference in follow-ups.

## Response times

Free and Starter plans receive support on a best-effort basis, typically within 2 business days. Pro includes priority email support with a 1-business-day target. Enterprise includes 24/5 support with a 1-hour first-response target for P1 incidents (workspace down or data loss) and 4 business hours for P2. Enterprise support hours are 24 hours a day Monday through Friday, plus weekend coverage for P1 incidents.

## Escalation

If a ticket is not progressing, reply with "escalate" and the ticket is reviewed by a support lead within 4 business hours. Enterprise customers can also contact their dedicated success manager directly.

## Status page

Live platform status and incident history are published at status.solstice.app. Subscribe there for email or webhook notifications; we do not send proactive incident emails to all customers.
